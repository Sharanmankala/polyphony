The core concept
Polyphony — spatially-aware creative AI director
Understands what it sees. Decomposes it into meaningful zones. Directs you through each one with voice, vision, and touch — simultaneously. Not a tool you operate. A director that guides the session.
Input layer — full multimodal
Voice
Gemini Live-style · real-time · "make the glasses rounder"
Vision
Image · video · screen · live camera feed
Touch/click
Tap a zone directly · drag to adjust · pinch to zoom
Text
Chat alongside · reference uploads · style prompts
Spatial intelligence layer — the new core
Scene understanding
BLIP2 + CLIP · identifies objects, people, zones · understands what matters in the frame
Zone decomposition
Breaks scene into editable regions · face → accessories → torso → hands → background · priority ordering
Context per zone
Each zone gets its own style options · knows glasses vs jacket vs shoes have different parameters
Director layer — the AI art director
Session orchestrator
LangGraph agent · decides which zone to tackle next · remembers what was changed · maintains creative coherence across the whole piece
Suggestion engine
Proactively offers options · "these 3 lens colors work with the jacket" · style consistency checks · "this clashes with what you changed earlier"
Execution layer — the Polyphony pillars
FRAME
Visual editing · inpainting · Real-ESRGAN · style transfer · zoom/crop · super-res on active zone
RESONANCE
Audio sync to edits · score matching · voice commands processed · denoise · enhance
SENTINEL
Monitors pipeline health · logs every edit decision · "undo to 3 steps ago" · error recovery
How a session actually feels — costume edit example
1
Upload. User drops image of man in costume. App parses the scene in 2–3 seconds. Identifies: face, glasses, jacket, gloves, belt, boots. AI
2
Director speaks. "I can see 6 editable zones. Want to start with the face?" App auto-zooms to face. voice vision
3
Glasses zone active. Shows 6 frame styles, 8 lens colors, 3 shape options — all rendered onto the face in real-time as user hovers. touch AI
4
User says "rounder, darker lenses." App applies. Asks "Keep the gold frame or try silver?" voice
5
Zone complete. Director moves. "Glasses done. Moving to hands — I see gloves, want to change material or color?" Auto-zooms to hands. voice vision
6
Coherence check. After all zones done — "The gold belt doesn't match the silver glasses you chose. Want me to fix that?" AI
Why nobody has built this
Adobe / Photoshop
You find the tool. No spatial awareness. No direction. No coherence across zones.
Runway / Firefly
Generate or inpaint. No zone decomposition. No session memory. No art direction.
Gemini Live
Conversational. Not spatially decomposed. Can't zoom, suggest, check fit per zone.
